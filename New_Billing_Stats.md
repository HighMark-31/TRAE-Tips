## 📊 Summary of Consumption

- **Capacity Equivalence**: Our tests show that **500 high-intensity sessions** are technically equivalent to the **600 requests** of the previous system. 
- **Complexity Impact**: The slightly lower number of sessions (500 vs 600) is justified by the extreme complexity of the tests: the data reflects heavy usage on full-stack repositories with **over 200 files**, where massive context and multi-turn reasoning were the standard.
- **Total Managed Spend**: The $150 managed spend over 12 days represents a high-intensity professional workflow.
- **Model Distribution**: Gemini-3-Pro-Preview accounts for over 50% of the total cost due to high reasoning depth, while Flash models process the majority of volume at a significantly lower cost.
- **Optimization Opportunities**: The 311 identified "inefficient blocks" suggest that even power users can reduce token burn through better prompt engineering and context management.
