# ❌ Current Major Issues

## 1️⃣ GPT-Series & Gemini 3 removed from US region

GPT-Series and Gemini 3 models have been removed from the US region.

There has been:

* No clear replacement
* No roadmap explanation
* No compensation plan for affected annual users

> [!NOTE]
> At the moment the team has confirmed that they have been removed but recommends contacting the official US support email: ussupport@mail.traeai.us.

For users who paid yearly for Advanced access, this creates serious value reduction.

---

## 2️⃣ Advanced Models will be removed (24 February)

From 24 February, Advanced models are expected to be removed.

This is the biggest concern.

Many users purchased:

> “Unlimited Advanced models”

Removing them mid-subscription:

* Changes the purchased benefits
* Reduces value
* Breaks expectations

If removal is necessary for legal or regulatory reasons, a replacement must be provided.

Removing without replacing is the real issue.

> [!NOTE]
> The team has not yet responded to this request at this time.

---

## 3️⃣ Missing Models in Global Version

The following models are not available in the international version:

* GPT-5.3 Codex
* GLM-5
* Doubao Seed Code 2.0
* Qwen 3.5
* Gemini 3.1

Community expectation:
Faster integration and clear explanations.

> [!NOTE]
> The team currently states that Doubao Seed Code 2.0 cannot be included in the international version due to legal compliance reasons. It's unclear whether they're working on a possible GLM release for the international version.
However, they haven't been able to provide any details regarding the new Qwen 3.5 & Gemini 3.1 model at this time.

---

## 4️⃣ GLM-5 not selectable in Custom Models

Even where mentioned, GLM-5 is not listed inside custom model settings.

This suggests:

* Incomplete rollout
* Backend limitation
* Or internal restriction

Clarification is required.

> [!NOTE]
> The team has not yet responded to or resolved this issue.

---

## 5️⃣ Compact Feature Triggering Too Often

The Compact feature is being triggered abnormally.

Reported issues:

* Triggered even for small prompts
* Causes unnecessary summarization
* Interrupts workflow
* Feels poorly tuned

This appears to be a threshold or logic problem.

> [!NOTE]
> The team has not yet responded to or resolved this issue.
