# GPT availability on TRAE (US Region)

## ❌ Current situation

At the moment, **TRAE has removed OpenAI (GPT) models from the US region** and from user access.

This decision was taken for **internal reasons** and is **not related to technical issues or model instability**.

---

## 🧭 Why this happened

Based on verification and current information, this appears to be a **pre-policy decision**.

The goal is to **prevent potential future disruptions** in case OpenAI decides to **restrict or terminate access to its models** due to **political reasons** or increasing **tensions between US and Chinese big tech companies**.

In short:
- TRAE is acting **proactively**
- The removal is meant to **avoid sudden service interruptions**
- This is a **risk-mitigation move**, not a permanent shutdown

---

## 🌍 Geopolitical context

The AI ecosystem is increasingly affected by **geopolitical dynamics**.  
Given the ongoing tensions between **US-based companies** and **Chinese tech firms**, access to certain AI models may become unstable or subject to sudden policy changes.

TRAE’s choice reflects an effort to **maintain platform stability and continuity** for users.

---

## 🔁 Alternatives inside TRAE

While GPT models are currently unavailable, TRAE continues to support and invest in **alternative models** that remain fully accessible and stable across regions.

These alternatives ensure that users can continue working without interruption.

---

## Summary

- OpenAI (GPT) models are currently **removed from TRAE in the US region**
- The decision was made for **internal and strategic reasons**
- It is a **pre-policy measure** to avoid possible future access blocks
- The cause is likely related to **political and geopolitical tensions**
- TRAE prioritizes **stability and long-term reliability**
